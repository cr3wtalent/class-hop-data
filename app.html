Before you go

Tap any class to open its sign up page at the studio in a new tab. You'll sign in with your Mindbody account for that studio to book and pay. If a class says "Find at studio", it opens that studio's schedule for the day instead. Some studios only open booking about a week ahead, so classes further out may say "Find at studio" until booking opens.

Tap a teacher's name to see all of their classes, or their @handle to open their Instagram and check out their style before you go. Teachers without a handle haven't been matched to an account yet.

Studios confirm teachers the day before, so subs happen. Check the studio's app or site before you head out, and book ahead where you can, since popular classes sell out.

* Millennium doesn't list a level on most classes. The studio says they run at an intermediate to advanced level unless the class name says otherwise.

Workshops, masterclasses and pop ups come from each studio's booking system and refresh every night. They're often priced separately, and class cards may not cover them.

Not included: cancelled classes, kids and youth classes, open studio blocks, and fitness classes like Pilates, yoga and stretch. Eighty Eight is listed as two studios: North Hollywood (NoHo) and Melrose.

Peridance books through its own website, so its classes say "Find at studio" and open Peridance's open class schedule, where you can sign up.

Levels are shown the way each studio names them on the class, so "Adv Beg" (advanced beginner) is grouped with Beg/Int. Spotlight, guest and pop up classes are special sessions with visiting teachers, companies and agencies, and they may be priced separately.

Not included: cancelled classes, kids and youth classes, livestreams, private lessons, crew rehearsals, and fitness classes like Pilates, yoga and stretch.

About Class Hop

Class Hop is an independent schedule guide from CR3W Talent LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any studio scheduling software or any studio listed. Class times, teachers and details come from each studio's own public online schedule, and are refreshed nightly. Schedules can change at any time, so always confirm with the studio before you go. All bookings, payments, cancellations and waivers are handled directly by the studio, not by CR3W Talent. Teacher Instagram links are shared for discovery only and may not always be current. Studio names, class names and trademarks belong to their owners. Your Class Hop subscription covers access to this guide only and does not include any classes. Are you a studio or teacher who would like a listing changed or removed? Email claire@cr3wtalent.com and we will take care of it promptly.
